Tm Docs Reader shows the docs of the Tallmaker extensions installed on your tenant, inside a Qlik Sense app. Add it to a sheet and it lists the installed products that ship docs, with contents, pages and search.

Each extension carries its docs in its own zip, so the reader always shows the docs of the installed version. Lock the reader to one product to give a demo app its own help sheet. The reader is free, reads no licence and is English only.

In embedded pages and mashups the reader cannot read other extensions and says so. Tallmaker extensions add their docs at their next release.
