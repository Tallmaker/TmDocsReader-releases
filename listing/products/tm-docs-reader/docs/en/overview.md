Tm Docs Reader brings help for your installed Tallmaker extensions into your Qlik Sense app.

Browse a product's pages or search for help across installed products that include docs. The reader is free and shows English docs for the installed version of each extension.

## Start here

- [When to use](when-to-use.md): decide whether the reader fits your app.
- [Getting started](getting-started.md): add a reader to a sheet and open your first page.
- [Settings](settings.md): focus on one product or choose an opening page.
- [Tips](tips.md): find help with missing products, pages or search results.
