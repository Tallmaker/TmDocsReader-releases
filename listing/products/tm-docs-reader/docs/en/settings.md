## Product

Choose one product to lock the reader to its docs. The reader then skips the product index and opens the contents of that product. Search finds only that product's pages.

Leave **All installed products** to show every installed Tallmaker product that ships docs.

A locked reader suits a demo app for one extension: put the reader on a help sheet of that app.

## Start page

Opens a page first. Write it as `product/page`, for example `tm-docs-reader/settings`. A start page that does not exist opens the contents of its product.
